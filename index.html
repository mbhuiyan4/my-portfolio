01 About

I'm a doctoral researcher at the University of Wisconsin–Madison working on the monitoring, modeling, and visualization of structures. My current work focuses on finite element modeling of an IH-94 skewed highway bridge and steel–concrete composite beams, and on structural health monitoring of bridges using distributed fiber optic sensing.

Before Madison, I spent nearly five years as a Lecturer at Daffodil International University in Dhaka, teaching Numerical Methods, Finite Element Methods, and Engineering Mathematics. My earlier research at BUET centered on the post-fire residual capacity of reinforced concrete members.

I enjoy turning slow, repetitive modeling tasks into reusable scripts. Abaqus scripting and user subroutines are a big part of my toolkit.

05 Python Projects

↗

3D RC Beam Post-Fire Simulation

Abaqus script that generates 3D reinforced concrete beams of any size with temperature-dependent material properties, then automates meshing, job creation, and analysis. It extracts load capacity, displacement, and peak rebar temperature to CSV.

  • Python
  • Abaqus
↗

Steel Beams Under Fire

Models 3D steel beams under the ISO 834 standard fire. Automates job creation, submission, and temperature monitoring at thermocouple locations, with configurable mesh size and section dimensions for parametric studies.

  • Python
  • Abaqus
  • ISO 834
C↗ S↗

Post-Fire Material Property Generators

Scripts that generate temperature-dependent concrete damage plasticity and steel plasticity properties for the Abaqus Material module, customizable to user-defined criteria.

  • Python
  • Abaqus CDP
↗

2D Truss Analysis Automation

Analyzes determinate and indeterminate trusses with the direct stiffness method. It takes node coordinates, connectivity, supports, and loads, and solves for displacements, bar forces, and stresses.

  • Python
  • NumPy
  • Stiffness Method
